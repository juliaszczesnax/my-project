# Nomad Talks — Calendly Setup Guide
### Two event types. Set up in 30 minutes. Ready to book today.

---

## EVENT 1 — "Nomad Talks — Quick Chat (15 min)"

**Purpose:** Pre-qualifying call before committing to a full episode. Vibe check for both sides.

---

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. Log in to Calendly → click **+ Create** → **Event Type** → **One-on-One**

2. **Event name:** `Nomad Talks — Quick Chat (15 min)`

3. **Duration:** 15 minutes

4. **Location:** Set to **Zoom** (or whatever you use for quick calls) or `Phone call — I'll call you`

5. **Description (shown on booking page):**
   > Hey! Really happy you're open to being on Nomad Talks. This 15-min chat is just a casual conversation — no pressure, no pitch. We'll see if your story is a good fit for the Voyage series and make sure you're comfortable with how it all works. See you soon! 🌍

6. **Availability:** Set your preferred time blocks (e.g. Mon–Fri, 10am–6pm your timezone)

7. **Buffer time:** Add 5 min after each event (so you have time to update Notion)

8. **Questions to ask on booking form:**
   - *"What's your Instagram handle or LinkedIn URL?"* (Short answer, required)
   - *"In one sentence, what's your story about?"* (Short answer, required)

9. Click **Save & Close**

## EVENT 2 — "Nomad Talks — Voyage Recording (60 min)"

**Purpose:** The actual podcast recording on Riverside.fm. Only send this link after the pre-chat.

---

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. In Calendly → **+ Create** → **Event Type** → **One-on-One**

2. **Event name:** `Nomad Talks — Voyage Recording (60 min)`

3. **Duration:** 60 minutes

4. **Location:** Set to **Custom** → enter:
   > We'll record on Riverside.fm. I'll send you a direct browser link before the session — no downloads needed.

5. **Description (shown on booking page):**
   > This is it — your Voyage episode. 🎙️ We'll record for about 45-60 minutes on Riverside.fm (just a browser link, no app needed). Make sure you've got earphones, good lighting, and a quiet space. Check the Prep Doc I sent you for the full checklist. Super excited for this one.

6. **Availability:** Set your recording slots (recommend specific blocks, e.g. Tue & Thu 2pm–6pm — keeps your schedule clean)

7. **Buffer time:** Add 30 min after each event (for file download + initial notes)

8. **Minimum scheduling notice:** Set to **48 hours** (gives you time to prep)

9. **Questions to ask on booking form:**
   - *"What's your bio? (3-5 bullet points about who you are and what you do)"* (Long answer, required)
   - *"Confirm: you have earphones + good lighting for the recording"* (Short answer, required)
   - *"Any topics you definitely want to cover or avoid?"* (Short answer, optional)

10. Click **Save & Close**
