# Nomad Talks — Episode Question Bank
### Structured for a 45-60 min conversation. Never read these out loud — know them, feel them, use them when they fit.

> **The two signature questions (always use both):**
> - *"What is your why?"*
> - *"What is your 'I made it' moment — that peak moment where you felt like this is actually real?"*

---

## SECTION 1 — OPEN (0–10 min)
*Goal: Get comfortable, warm up, let the guest hear their own voice. Don't go deep yet.*

1. So — set the scene for me. Where in the world are you right now, and what does a normal Tuesday look like for you?
2. Give me the quick version of your story — who were you before all this, and who are you now?
3. Where did you grow up, and what was the world you were raised in like? Was "freedom" even a concept on your radar back then?
4. When you were a teenager, what did you think your life was going to look like?
5. At what point did you first realise that the "normal" path wasn't going to work for you?
6. Is there one thing — a moment, a conversation, a book — that you'd say was the spark?
7. What was your life on paper right before you made the jump? Job, city, routine — what were you leaving?

---

## SECTION 2 — MIDDLE (10–40 min)
*Goal: The real story. Journey, money, struggles, turning points. This is the meat — let it breathe.*

### The Jump
8. How did you actually make the jump? Was it a planned transition or a "I'm done, I'm out" moment?
9. What did the people closest to you say when you told them what you were doing?
10. Did you have savings? A plan? Or did you just go?
11. What was the first month like — honestly?

### Money & Work
12. How did you start making money? Walk me through it practically.
13. What did your income look like in the first year? Be real — I think people need to hear the actual numbers or at least the shape of it.
14. What's your model now? How do you generate income — what does that actually look like day to day?
15. What's been the biggest financial mistake you've made, and what did it cost you?
16. Is there a moment where you felt financially free for the first time — not rich, just free?

### The Nomad / Freedom Life
17. How many places have you lived or worked from? What's been your favourite and why?
18. What does "location freedom" actually feel like in practice — does it live up to the idea of it?
19. What's the loneliest it's ever gotten? Because no one talks about that side.
20. How do you build community when you're always moving?
21. What does your routine look like — do you have one, or is that the whole point?

### Struggles & Real Talk
22. What's been the hardest chapter of this journey? The one you don't really post about?
23. Have you ever wanted to quit and go back? What was that moment?
24. What's the biggest misconception people have about the nomad/freelance life?
25. What does failure look like in your world — have you had one that really hit?
26. What's something you gave up to build this life that you still think about?

### Mindset & Growth
27. How has your relationship with fear changed over time?
28. **What is your why?** Not the Instagram version — the real one. Why are you actually doing this?
29. Who or what has had the biggest impact on how you think?
30. What's a belief you had 3 years ago that you've completely changed your mind on?
31. What's the skill — not a business skill, a human skill — that's made the biggest difference for you?

---

## SECTION 3 — POWER QUESTIONS (40–55 min)
*Goal: The emotional, memorable moments. Slow down. Let them think. Don't rush the answer.*

32. **What is your "I made it" moment?** That peak moment where you genuinely felt — this is actually real. This is working.
33. If you could go back and give one piece of advice to yourself the week before the jump — what would it be?
34. What's something you've built or done that you're genuinely proud of — not because of what it earned, but because of what it meant?
35. Is there a person in your life who's seen this whole journey unfold — and what do you think they'd say about who you've become?
36. What does success actually mean to you now, compared to what it meant before?
37. What's a moment where you thought you'd failed, but it turned out to be exactly what you needed?
38. If Nomad Talks reaches one person who's exactly where you were before all this — what's the one thing you want them to walk away with from your story?
39. What are you most afraid of right now? Not back then — right now.
40. What does your life need to look like in 5 years for you to say: "I lived it the way I wanted"?

---

## SECTION 4 — CLOSE (55–60 min)
*Goal: Wrap it up with energy. Leave on a high. Give them a moment to share their work.*

### Rapid Fire (pick 3–5)
41. One book that changed how you see the world?
42. Best country you've ever worked from?
43. Overrated destination that everyone talks about?
44. Biggest money mindset shift you've had in one sentence?
45. Morning person or night owl — and has that changed since going nomad?
46. The one tool or app you could not live without?
47. What's something totally non-work-related that makes you happy right now?
48. One person — alive or not — whose brain you'd want to pick for a day?

### Final Questions
49. What are you working on or building right now? What's the next chapter?
50. For someone listening who feels stuck and wants to start — what's the very first step they should take? Not the motivational version, the practical one.
51. Where can people follow your journey and find your work? Give us all the handles.

---

## SECTION 5 — BONUS (use if conversation stalls or goes deep)
*Pull from these if a specific thread opens up.*

**On entrepreneurship:**
- Did you ever run a "business" before this that completely flopped? What happened?
- How do you think about pricing your work — what shifted your confidence there?
- What's the difference between a freelancer and a business owner in your mind?

**On travel and culture:**
- Has living in different cultures changed your values in ways you didn't expect?
- What's the most uncomfortable you've ever been — and was it worth it?
- What does "home" mean to you now?

**On relationships:**
- How has this lifestyle affected your closest relationships?
- Have you had to let go of friendships that couldn't keep up with who you were becoming?
- What does a healthy relationship look like with someone who's always moving?

**On social media and the creator side:**
- Do you think social media has been honest about what the nomad life actually looks like?
- Have you ever felt pressure to perform the lifestyle rather than just live it?
- What's one thing you'd never post about — and why?

---

## INTERVIEW NOTES FOR YOU

- **Use silence.** When someone finishes a powerful answer, pause for 2 seconds before asking the next question. It shows you're listening — and sometimes they add the best line right there.
- **Follow the energy.** If a guest lights up on a topic, go there — even if it's not on the list.
- **Don't read questions.** Know the sections, have the bank open as a reference. But look at them, not the screen.
- **The two signature questions are non-negotiable.** *"What is your why?"* and *"What is your I made it moment?"* — these are in every Voyage, no exceptions.
- **End strong.** Always close with the practical advice question (Q50) — it's what the audience remembers.
