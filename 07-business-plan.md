# Nomad Talks — Business Plan & Full SOP
### By Julia (@juliaszczesnax)

---

## THE BIG PICTURE

Nomad Talks is a dual-purpose operation.

**Purpose 1:** Build a genuine interview podcast for people who want a life of freedom. Real stories, real numbers, real talk. No fluff, no highlight reels.

**Purpose 2:** Act as a warm lead pipeline for Julia's SMMA business (social media and branding packages). Every guest is a potential client. Every published episode is a trust-building asset.

The two purposes reinforce each other. A great podcast builds credibility. Credibility makes the SMMA pitch effortless.

---

## THE PRODUCT

**The Podcast:** Nomad Talks
**Episode format:** Voyage 01, Voyage 02... (no end number — built to scale)
**Length:** 45-60 minutes
**Recording platform:** Riverside.fm
**Distribution:** YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts
**Social cut:** 60-90 second Reel on Instagram (guest added as collaborator)
**Posting frequency:** Release when ready — quality over schedule

---

## THE TWO REVENUE STREAMS

### Stream 1 — SMMA (Primary)
- **What you sell:** Social media management and branding packages
- **How the podcast feeds it:** Every guest is a founder, freelancer, or creator who likely needs social media help. The podcast creates a relationship before any pitch happens. You're not a cold salesperson — you're the person who gave them a platform.
- **The window:** After the episode publishes, you have a natural, warm reason to follow up.

### Stream 2 — Podcast Growth (Future)
- Sponsorships once you hit consistent downloads
- Brand partnerships with nomad/travel/remote work tools
- Community or course built off the audience

## THE FULL CUSTOMER JOURNEY

### STAGE 0 — DISCOVERY
**What happens:** Julia finds a potential guest.
**Where:** Instagram, LinkedIn, referrals from past guests, podcast listeners
**Who to look for:**
- Freelancers, nomads, founders, solopreneurs with a real story
- People who've made a non-traditional life work financially
- Micro-creators (1k-50k followers) — they're more accessible and their audiences are often more engaged
- Founders who run small businesses and likely need social media help (SMMA angle)

**Green flags for a good guest:**
- They post about freedom, money, travel, building a business
- They have a journey — before and after
- They're active on Instagram or LinkedIn
- They seem real, not performative

**Red flags:**
- Only posts aesthetic content with no substance
- Has a manager or PR rep (too complicated at this stage)
- Obviously just wants exposure, no real story to tell

**Notion action:** Add to CRM → Pipeline Stage = **Prospecting**

### STAGE 1 — OUTREACH
**What happens:** Julia sends a personalised first DM via Instagram or LinkedIn.
**Template:** Template A (`01-message-templates.md`)
**Key:** Personalise the `[SPECIFIC THING]` line — one genuine sentence about why them. Don't skip this.

**Tips that increase reply rate:**
- Send on Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-12pm in their timezone if possible
- Engage with one of their posts the day before (like or a genuine comment)
- Keep it short — if it's longer than 5 lines they won't read it
- Don't follow up more than once if they don't reply (respect their time)

### STAGE 3 — THE PRE-CHAT (15 minutes)
**What happens:** Casual video/voice call. Vibe check for both sides.

**Your agenda (don't share this — just keep it in your head):**

1. **0-3 min:** Small talk. Where are they, what are they working on right now?
2. **3-8 min:** Ask them to give you the 2-minute version of their story. Listen for the hook — the thing that makes their journey interesting.
3. **8-12 min:** Explain Nomad Talks briefly. The Voyage format. How it works. The Reel after.
4. **12-15 min:** Gauge fit. Do they have enough depth for 60 mins? Do you actually like talking to them? Would your audience care?

**During the call, quietly assess:**
- SMMA lead potential: Do they have a business that needs social media? Do they seem to be struggling with content or growth? Are they doing their own social badly? → Mark as Hot or Warm in Notion after the call.
- Guest fit: Is this someone who will open up on mic? Are they articulate? Is their story specific enough to fill 60 minutes?

**Notion action after call:** Pipeline Stage = **Pre-Chat Done** | Update SMMA Lead Potential | Add notes while fresh (even 2-3 lines)

### STAGE 5 — RECORDING DAY

**30 min before:**
- Send guest the Riverside.fm session link via DM or the Calendly confirmation email
- Save the link to Notion → Recording Link field
- Do your own gear check: headphones, camera, good light, quiet space, water

**At the start of the session:**
- Let them arrive first, give them 2-3 mins to settle
- Do a quick audio check together ("Say something — I'll check your levels")
- Brief them: "We'll just have a conversation, I'll ask questions, no wrong answers, if you want to rephrase anything just say so and we'll keep going"
- Start recording in Riverside.fm

**During the recording — episode structure:**
| Time | Section | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 min | OPEN | Warm up, intro the guest, set the scene |
| 10-40 min | MIDDLE | Their journey, money, struggles, turning points |
| 40-55 min | POWER QUESTIONS | Emotional moments, the "why", the "I made it" moment |
| 55-60 min | CLOSE | Rapid fire, advice, where to find them |

**Two questions that must appear in every episode:**
- "What is your why?"
- "What is your 'I made it' moment?"

**After the session:**
- Download the raw files from Riverside.fm immediately (video + audio separately)
- Back them up to a second location (Google Drive, external drive)
- Jot 3-5 bullet points of notes while the conversation is fresh — best moments, timestamps if you remember them, anything for the Reel

**Notion action:** Pipeline Stage = **Recorded** | Record Date confirmed

### STAGE 6 — EDITING & DISTRIBUTION

#### Step 1 — Edit the full episode
- Import Riverside.fm files into your editing software
- Remove: dead air, filler at start/end, obvious stumbles (only if they asked you to)
- Keep: natural pauses, laughs, imperfect sentences — this is what makes it feel real
- Add: intro music/jingle, your intro voiceover, outro
- Export: audio (MP3, 128kbps minimum) + video (1080p minimum)

#### Step 2 — Cut the Reel
- Find the single best 60-90 second moment from the episode
- Best Reel moments: a strong opinion, a vulnerable confession, a counterintuitive take, or the "I made it" answer
- Add captions (Riverside.fm has auto-captions, or use CapCut)
- Format: vertical 9:16 for Instagram Reels

#### Step 3 — Write the content

**Episode title (YouTube/Spotify/Apple):**
Format: `[Guest Name]: [The Hook] | Nomad Talks Voyage [##]`
Example: `Maria Silva: Left a €80k job with €2k saved and never looked back | Nomad Talks Voyage 03`

**YouTube description (template):**
```
[Guest first name] [one-line bio — their story in plain language].

In this Voyage we talk about:
• [Topic 1]
• [Topic 2]
• [Topic 3]

Follow [Guest Name]:
Instagram: [handle]
[Other links]

Follow Nomad Talks:
Instagram: @juliaszczesnax
[YouTube channel link]
[Spotify link]
[Apple Podcasts link]

Chapters:
00:00 Intro
[Add timestamps after editing]
```

**Instagram Reel caption:**
Keep it short. One punchy line from the episode as the hook, then 2-3 lines of context, then tag the guest.
Example:
> "She left her €80k salary with €2k in savings and a one-way ticket. No plan B."
>
> Full story on Nomad Talks — link in bio.
>
> @guesthandle 🌍

#### Step 4 — Distribute

| Platform | What to upload | How |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Full episode (video) | Upload as unlisted first, check it, then publish |
| Spotify | Audio file (MP3) | Via RSS feed or direct upload through Spotify for Podcasters |
| Apple Podcasts | Audio file (MP3) | Via RSS feed (same feed as Spotify if using a host like Buzzsprout/Anchor) |
| Instagram | Reel (vertical video) | Post + add guest as collaborator |

**Recommended podcast hosting:** Buzzsprout or Spotify for Podcasters (free tier) — upload once and it distributes to Spotify, Apple, and more automatically.

#### Step 5 — Post and notify

- Post the Reel on Instagram
- Add guest as **collaborator** (they must accept — send them a DM heads up)
- Send the guest a DM with all the links: YouTube, Spotify, Apple, Instagram Reel
- Update Notion: Published URL, Publish Date, Pipeline Stage = **Published** ✅

### STAGE 7 — SMMA FOLLOW-UP

This happens 2-5 days after the episode is live. Only for guests marked **Hot** or **Warm** in Notion.

**The approach:** Don't pitch. Reference the episode. Make it feel natural.

**Message template:**
> "Hey [name]! Really glad the episode is live — the response has been great. I've been thinking about your story and honestly, the way you're building [their thing] is exactly the kind of brand that should be everywhere on social right now.
>
> Social media and branding is actually what I do for founders and creators — I run a small SMMA. Happy to jump on a quick call if you ever want to talk through what that could look like for you. No pressure at all."

**Key rules:**
- Never pitch before the episode is live
- Always reference something specific from their story
- One message only — don't follow up on the pitch itself
- If they say yes, move them into your SMMA pipeline

**Notion action:** Mark **SMMA Follow-up Done?** ✅

## WHAT MAKES THIS WORK LONG-TERM

Three things keep this alive past Voyage 10:

**1. Guest quality over quantity.** One great guest who opens up and tells a real story is worth more than five polished guests who say nothing memorable. Be selective.

**2. The SMMA angle is invisible.** The podcast should never feel like a sales funnel to guests or the audience. The sales happen in private, after the relationship is built. Keep it that way.

**3. The Reel is the growth engine.** The full episode builds depth. The Reel builds reach. Every episode should have one Reel cut that could make someone stop scrolling. Find that moment every time.
