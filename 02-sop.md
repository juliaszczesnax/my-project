# Nomad Talks — Standard Operating Procedure
### The Voyage Playbook: From Finding a Guest to Publishing

> One repeatable system. Every episode. Works solo or with a VA.
> Built for Voyage 01 — still works at Voyage 50.

---

## THE VOYAGE CHECKLIST

### 🔍 PHASE 1 — PROSPECT
- [ ] 1. Find a potential guest (Instagram, LinkedIn, referrals, podcast listeners)
- [ ] 2. Qualify them mentally: *Do they have a real story about freedom, freelancing, nomad life, or entrepreneurship?*
- [ ] 3. Add them to Notion → set Pipeline Stage to **Prospecting**
- [ ] 4. Fill in: Guest Name, Instagram Handle or LinkedIn URL, Outreach Channel, SMMA Lead Potential

---

### 📩 PHASE 2 — OUTREACH
- [ ] 5. Send **Template A** via Instagram DM or LinkedIn message (personalise the [SPECIFIC THING] line)
- [ ] 6. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Contacted**
- [ ] 7. Add note in Notion: date sent, which platform, what you personalised

---

### 📅 PHASE 3 — PRE-CHAT BOOKING
- [ ] 8. If they say yes → send **Template B** with Calendly Event 1 link (15-min Quick Chat)
- [ ] 9. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Pre-Chat Booked**

---

### 🎙️ PHASE 4 — PRE-CHAT
- [ ] 10. Run the 15-min call: vibe check, hear their story, confirm they're a good fit
- [ ] 11. Assess SMMA lead potential during the call → update Notion (Hot / Warm / Not a fit)
- [ ] 12. Add personal notes to Notion Notes field
- [ ] 13. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Pre-Chat Done**

---

### 📬 PHASE 5 — RECORDING BOOKING
- [ ] 14. If both say yes → send **Template C** with Calendly Event 2 link (60-min Recording)
- [ ] 15. Send the **Guest Prep Doc** (see `05-guest-prep-doc.md`)
- [ ] 16. Mark **Prep Doc Sent?** checkbox in Notion ✅
- [ ] 17. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Podcast Booked**
- [ ] 18. Assign **Voyage Number** and draft **Episode Title** in Notion

---

### 🎬 PHASE 6 — RECORDING DAY
- [ ] 19. Send guest the Riverside.fm session link 30 min before (add to Notion **Recording Link** field)
- [ ] 20. Quick gear check at start: audio, lighting, headphones
- [ ] 21. Record the episode on Riverside.fm (~45-60 min)
- [ ] 22. Download raw files immediately after the session
- [ ] 23. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Recorded**
- [ ] 24. Log the **Record Date** in Notion

---

### ✂️ PHASE 7 — EDITING & PUBLISHING
- [ ] 25. Edit full episode (remove dead air, clean audio, add intro/outro)
- [ ] 26. Cut a 60-90 sec Reel from the best moment
- [ ] 27. Write episode description + show notes
- [ ] 28. Upload full episode to YouTube
- [ ] 29. Post Reel on Instagram → add guest as **collaborator**
- [ ] 30. Fill in **Published URL** in Notion, set **Publish Date**
- [ ] 31. Update Notion Pipeline Stage → **Published** 🎉

---

### 💼 PHASE 8 — SMMA FOLLOW-UP (if applicable)
- [ ] 32. If SMMA Lead Potential = **Hot** or **Warm** → follow up within 3 days of publishing
- [ ] 33. Reference the episode naturally: *"Really loved having you on — your story hit hard. I also do social media management for founders/nomads. Happy to share more if that's ever useful."*
- [ ] 34. Mark **SMMA Follow-up Done?** checkbox in Notion ✅

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notion | CRM + Episode Tracker |
| Calendly Event 1 | 15-min Pre-Chat booking |
| Calendly Event 2 | 60-min Recording booking |
| Riverside.fm | Recording platform |
| Instagram / LinkedIn | Outreach channels |

---

> **Rule of thumb:** Never let a guest sit in one Notion stage for more than 5 days without a touchpoint.
