## What is Nomad Talks?

**Nomad Talks** is an interview podcast for people who feel stuck and want to build a life of freedom — location freedom, financial freedom, doing work that actually means something.

Each episode is called a **Voyage**. Think of it like a journey story — your journey.

The audience: young people figuring out how to escape the 9-5, build a freelance life, or go nomadic. They need to hear from people who've done it. People like you.

No fluff. No highlight reels. Real talk.

## What to Expect on the Day

- **Platform:** We record on **Riverside.fm** — it's browser-based, no app download needed. I'll send you a direct link ~30 minutes before we start.
- **Duration:** About **45-60 minutes**. It'll go faster than you think.
- **Format:** Relaxed conversation. I ask questions, you talk — naturally. There's no script, no "right answers." I might go off-script if something interesting comes up. That's a good thing.
- **Vibe:** Think of it like catching up with someone you just met but already vibe with. Not an interview. A conversation.
- **If you mess up:** Don't worry. We're not live. You can say "let me rephrase that" and we'll just keep going — I'll cut it in editing.

## Tech Checklist ✅

Please check all of these before we start. Bad audio is the only thing that can ruin an otherwise great episode.

- [ ] **Earphones or headphones** — this is the most important thing. Earbuds work fine. No laptop speakers.
- [ ] **Quiet space** — close the door, turn off fans/AC if possible, let people know you're recording
- [ ] **Good lighting** — natural light is perfect. Sit facing a window, not with it behind you.
- [ ] **Stable internet** — use WiFi or ethernet. Not a hotspot if you can avoid it.
- [ ] **Charged device** — or plugged in
- [ ] **Camera on** — we record video too. Doesn't need to be perfect, just present.
- [ ] **Test your setup** — click the Riverside link when you get it and do a quick audio check before we start

**If something goes wrong technically:** just message me on Instagram **[YOUR INSTAGRAM HANDLE]** and we'll sort it fast.

## What to Prepare

You don't need to prepare much. But there are two things that really help:

### 1. Your Bio (3-5 bullet points)
I'll use this to introduce you at the start of the episode. Keep it short and real — not a LinkedIn bio.

**What to include:**
- Who you are + where you're from
- What you do / how you make money
- Where you're based now (or have been)
- One thing that makes your story unusual or interesting
- Optional: something personal — a hobby, a value, a weird fact

**Example:**
- From São Paulo, now full-time nomad — 14 countries in 3 years
- Freelance brand designer for e-commerce startups
- Left a corporate marketing job with no plan — just a one-way ticket
- Big believer that you don't need to be rich to start, just ready
- Currently writing a Substack about building while travelling

**Send your bio to me** via DM or reply to my message before the session. Or paste it in the Riverside.fm chat when we start.

### 2. Your Social Handles
So I can tag you correctly in the Reel and episode post. Just Instagram and/or LinkedIn is fine.

## Questions You Might Be Asked

*This isn't a script — just a heads up so nothing catches you off guard. I'll go wherever the conversation naturally leads.*

**Opening:**
- Tell me your story — how did you get from [where you started] to where you are now?
- What was your life like before the freedom thing clicked?

**Your Journey:**
- What was the moment you decided to actually make the jump?
- How did you deal with the fear / the people who didn't get it?
- What did money look like in the early days? Be real about it.
- What's something most people don't talk about that you think they should?

**Mindset & Lessons:**
- What's your "why"? (This one always comes up)
- What's been your hardest moment on this journey?
- What do you know now that you wish you'd known at the start?

**The Big One:**
- What's your *"I made it"* moment — that peak moment where you genuinely felt like, this is actually real?

**Closing:**
- What's one piece of advice for someone who's exactly where you were before all this?
- What are you working on / building right now?
- Where can people follow your journey?
